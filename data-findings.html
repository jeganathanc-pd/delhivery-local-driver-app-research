Category A: Key Business Metrics

1. Fulfillment Rate is a Major Problem

  • Overall fulfillment: ~54.5% — target is >70%. Massive gap.
  • Dropped from ~58% in May → ~52-53% in June → recovered to ~55% in late July
  • Nearly half of all orders placed are NOT getting delivered

Research question: Why are orders not getting fulfilled? Drivers not accepting broadcasts? Accepting then cancelling? Not enough drivers online?

2. Active Driver Count is Growing, But Fulfillment Isn't

  • Drivers grew from ~8K (late April) to ~29K (late July)
  • Fulfillment didn't improve proportionally — more drivers didn't solve the problem
  • Issue isn't supply — it's driver behavior, app experience, or order-driver matching

Research question: If there are enough drivers, why aren't they accepting/completing orders? Is the broadcast UX failing them?

4. Pickup Time is Stable but Could Be Better

  • P80 Order to Pickup Arrival: ~21 minutes (target <15 min)
  • P80 Pickup Distance: ~3.1–3.6 km
  • At least 20% of pickups take longer than 21 minutes

Research question: What happens between acceptance and arriving at pickup? Finishing previous delivery? Far from pickup? Navigation issue?

5. Star vs Non-Star Flexi Drivers

  • Non-star Flexi riders: ~70-74% of fulfilled orders
  • Star Flexi riders: ~15-20%
  • Slot riders: ~8-11% (declining from ~9.5% to ~7.5%)

Research question: Do drivers understand Slots? Why are fewer using it?

6. WAU Drivers vs Delivered Orders

  • WAU grew from ~8K to ~29K
  • "Star" drivers are only ~2-5K out of 25-29K
  • Most drivers are "Non-star" — less reliable/active

Research question: What differentiates Star behavior? Can the app help Non-stars perform better?

28. Behavioral Patterns Behind Cancellations

Pattern A: "Gaming the system" (~19%)
Driver asks customer to cancel — avoids penalty on their record.

Q: Why won't they cancel themselves? Is the penalty too harsh?

Pattern B: "Distance surprise" (~23%)
Actual route (traffic, one-ways) much longer than straight-line distance in broadcast.

Q: Is broadcast showing straight-line vs actual route? Does navigation surprise them?

Pattern C: "Vehicle mismatch" (~20%)
Order shouldn't have come to this driver, or package bigger than described.

Q: Consumer misrepresenting size? System broadcasting to wrong vehicles?

Key Takeaways

  • 5% acceptance rate — 95% of broadcasts ignored. Broadcast UX needs rethinking.
  • Stars earn 2-3x more because they accept 2-3x more — willingness, not hours.
  • Non-Stars logging 5+ hrs but 0.3 deliveries/day — passive/backup usage.
  • Jaipur 20-30% vs Bangalore 3-7% acceptance — city dynamics matter hugely.
  • 4-5K drivers log <1 hr daily — understand why they leave.
  • 21% post-assignment cancellation — the app is failing at the decision-making moment.
