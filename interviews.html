Interview #1: Thanuj Kumar

Ops Team Aug 5, 2026

1. Onboarding & Training

Training Videos Exist — But Are Hidden

  • Training videos already exist but are stored in Google Drive
  • When a driver faces a problem and contacts ops, the team manually shares the relevant video link
  • Drivers can't discover or access these on their own
Opportunity: Surface training videos inside the app — contextual help at the point of confusion instead of relying on ops to manually share links.

Proposed Idea from Ops: Training as Enforcement

If a driver cancels too much → block them → require them to watch a training video + pass a quiz → then unblock.

(Not current system — a suggestion from ops as a use case for making training content actionable.)

New Drivers Need In-App Guidance

  • New users who are unfamiliar with the system need coachmarks or a starter helper
  • Goal: help them understand the app independently — without needing ops or support
  • Currently no in-app onboarding exists in the driver app (onboarding happens in a separate app)
Insight: Drivers download the app and are expected to figure it out on their own. Any confusion = call to support.

"In Progress" State is Useless

  • If onboarding is incomplete, the UI shows "in progress" — but doesn't say what is pending
  • Internally, the team knows the blocker (e.g., PAN still under verification) but this isn't shown to the driver
  • Result: drivers don't know what's wrong → they call support → it's treated as low priority by the Goa CX team
Opportunity: Show explicit status messages (e.g., "Your PAN is under review — expected 2–3 days") so drivers know the actual blocker without calling support.

2. App Usability Issues

Swipe Action Fails / Buffering

  • The "swipe to mark arrived/delivered" action sometimes doesn't register — buffering or loading issue
  • This is a core action failure — drivers can't complete their primary task

Drivers Call Ops When They Can't:

  • Mark as arrived
  • Mark as delivered
  • Find the customer's contact number
Note: These are basic, high-frequency actions. If drivers need to call ops for these, the app is failing at its primary job.

3. Slots Feature

Slots Work Well for 2-Wheelers

  • 2W drivers engage with slots and show up reliably
  • 70%+ of drivers who book slots actually show up

Tata Ace Drivers Avoid Slots

  • Auto-assign feature sends them to locations they don't want to go
  • They lose control over route selection when in a slot
  • This defeats the purpose — they'd rather stay Flexi and choose orders
Insight: Slots require giving up control. For Tata Ace drivers (longer routes, more effort per trip), the tradeoff doesn't work because auto-assign ignores their preferences.

Access is Restricted

  • Only Star drivers can access Slots (Non-Star cannot)
  • Two models exist: Flexi (default, per-order) vs Slots (opt-in, guaranteed earning)

4. Surge & Demand Visibility

Drivers Want a Demand Heatmap

  • Drivers want to know where high-demand/surge areas are — like Rapido shows on their map
  • Currently: ops team sends push notifications to drivers who are in surge-applied areas
  • Gap: no in-app visibility of demand zones — drivers can't proactively position themselves
Opportunity: Show surge/demand zones on the map in-app. Let drivers make informed decisions about where to go, instead of passively waiting for a push notification.

5. Driver Support Flow

Two Paths to Resolution

How a driver gets help depends on whether they personally know someone on the ops team:

DRIVER FACES AN ISSUE │ ├── Path 1: Doesn't know ops number (default) │ │ │ └── Calls support helpline │ │ │ └── CX Team (Goa) picks up │ │ │ ├── Solved ✓ │ │ │ └── Unsolved → Escalates to OPS Team (Bangalore) │ │ │ └── Ops checks CRN (order ID) │ │ │ └── Raises ticket in OS1 │ │ │ ├── Solved ✓ │ │ │ └── Needs escalation │ │ │ └── Shares in Google Chat with relevant team │ │ └── Path 2: Knows ops member's number (informal) │ └── Calls ops directly (skips CX) │ └── Ops checks CRN (order ID) │ └── Raises ticket in OS1 │ ├── Solved ✓ │ └── Needs escalation │ └── Shares in Google Chat with relevant team

Key Observation

Inequity in support access: Drivers with personal ops connections get faster resolution by skipping the CX queue entirely. Drivers without connections go through Goa CX first (where driver issues are treated as low priority), adding delay and frustration.
